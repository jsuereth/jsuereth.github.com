# About Me

<div class="bio-photo-wrapper">
  <img src="/resources/teaching.png" alt="Josh Suereth" class="bio-photo" />
</div>

I am a software engineer, open source maintainer, and author. I served on the **OpenTelemetry Technical Committee**, driving telemetry standards, semantic conventions, and observability architecture across the cloud-native ecosystem. 

Professionally, I work as a Senior Staff Software Engineer at Google, focusing on distributed systems and agent observability. 

Earlier in my career, I spent many years in the Scala and JVM ecosystem. I was a principal engineer at Typesafe (now Lightbend), the lead maintainer of **sbt** (the Simple Build Tool), and author of [*Scala in Depth*](https://www.manning.com/books/scala-in-depth) (2012) and co-author of [*sbt in Action*](https://www.manning.com/books/sbt-in-action) (2015).

My current technical focus centers on:
* **Observability by Design**: Building semantic convention frameworks and automated code generation via tools like [OpenTelemetry Weaver](https://github.com/open-telemetry/weaver).
* **Systems in Rust**: Exploring high-performance, low-overhead, safe, concurrent and observable systems.
* **Build and Tooling Ergonomics**: Improving developer velocity, process resiliency and security.
